Scientific Integrity

Sources and content notes

This prototype uses introductory textbook-level explanations of cell signaling. It was drafted with AI assistance; the app does not run a live AI model. The sources below are suggested references, not evidence that every statement was independently checked against them.

Suggested authoritative reading

  • OpenStax, Biology 2e — chapters covering cell communication and signal transduction (requires internet to open).
  • NCBI Bookshelf — freely available molecular and cell biology reference books (requires internet to open).
  • Cell Signaling Technology: Signaling Pathways — pathway-oriented educational reference (requires internet to open).

Scientific limitations

  • RTK activation is receptor-specific; not every receptor follows the same dimerization mechanism.
  • Grb2/SOS recruitment is a common, simplified EGFR–Ras route; alternate adaptor arrangements exist.
  • Ras is a small GTPase. Raf, MEK, and ERK are kinases with different positions in the cascade.
  • ERK outcomes depend on cellular context, signal duration, feedback, and crosstalk.
  • Lesson summaries and quiz items are educational simplifications, not medical advice.
